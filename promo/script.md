# Lunch Board: 75-second promo script

**Idea:** a 35-person team passes one phone around and orders by hand, and it takes a few hours every day.
Lunch Board turns that into about 5 minutes. Slow, simple, one idea per scene.

**Voices:** Narrator (calm, unhurried). Four teammates, heard once each in scene 2.
**Music:** a gentle minor loop with a ticking clock for the problem, then a bright, warm groove from
"Meet Lunch Board" to the end. The music dips under every spoken line.
**Captions:** every narrator line appears on screen, so the film works on mute.

---

## 1. The team (0:00 to 0:08)

**Picture:** eight colleagues hop into a line at the office counter. "35 people" counts up. A small
clock reads 12:00 PM. A thought bubble pops over each head as the dishes are named.

**Narrator:** Every day at noon, thirty-five people get hungry.
**Narrator:** Veg. Pizza. Burgers. Everyone wants something different.

**Sound:** clock ticks under the music; a soft pop for each thought bubble.

## 2. One phone, passed hand to hand (0:09 to 0:26)

**Picture:** the bubbles drift away. One person lights up with an "Organiser" tag, holding their
phone, while the rest fade back. The phone flies along an arc to the first teammate, who lights up
and says what they want. A check mark, then the phone moves on to the next person, and the next.
After four, the phone starts bouncing up and down the line in a blur, "Picks collected" races to
35 / 35 and the small clock in the corner grows and runs from 12:00 to 1:30 PM.

**Narrator:** So, one person becomes the organiser.
**Narrator:** They pass their phone round, so everyone can pick.
**Teammate 1:** A paneer roll for me, please.
**Teammate 2:** Is the biryani veg?
**Teammate 3:** No onions, please.
**Teammate 4:** Wait, what are you getting?
**Narrator:** And on and on, until all thirty-five have picked.

**Sound:** a soft swish and a tap each time the phone changes hands, a pop as each answer appears,
a marimba note for each check.

## 3. Ordering by hand (0:26 to 0:34)

**Picture:** one phone. Five orders slide in, one at a time, each turning from "Placing..." to
"Placed": biryani, pizza, burgers, dosa, ice cream. Then a big clock spins from 1:30 to 3:15 next
to "A few hours, every day". For a beat, the whole line looks tired: frowns, sweat, scribbles.

**Narrator:** Then they order by hand, one restaurant at a time.
**Narrator:** It takes a few hours. Every single day.

**Sound:** a small "ding" per order; the clock ticks speed up as the hands spin; a low note on "hours".

## 4. Why it's still hard (0:34 to 0:47)

**Picture:** the flow from the problem slide builds box by box, under the heading "How team lunch
works today": 35 people → One organiser → Orders by hand → A few hours. Two limits slide in below,
one per line of voice:
- **Limit 1. Swiggy Group Order:** everyone adds to one cart, but from one restaurant only.
- **Limit 2. Swiggy multi-restaurant checkout:** still one person's cart, up to 5 restaurants.

The organiser box gets a heavy outline and a "The bottleneck" tag.

**Narrator:** Thirty-five people. One organiser. Orders by hand.
**Narrator:** Swiggy's group order shares one cart, but only from one restaurant.
**Narrator:** And its multi-restaurant checkout is still one person's cart.

**Sound:** the music thins out; a riser builds into the next scene.

## 5. Meet Lunch Board (0:47 to 0:50)

**Picture:** a dark title card sweeps up. The tiffin draws in, then "Lunch Board" and "Everyone
picks. One order."

**Narrator:** Meet Lunch Board.

**Sound:** whoosh, a bright chime, and the music turns major and lifts.

## 6. How it works (0:50 to 1:00)

**Picture:** the real app, step by step, with a big step number for each.
1. **Everyone picks.** The "Pick my lunch" screen; a cursor taps "Complete my order".
2. **The app groups.** The cart sorted by restaurant, with the first restaurant's group and the
   "By restaurant" switch outlined in blue.
3. **The host confirms.** The bot fills one Swiggy cart, then the green "Order placed" screen.

**Narrator:** Everyone picks their own lunch, from any restaurant.
**Narrator:** The app groups the picks by restaurant.
**Narrator:** The host taps confirm, and the app places the order.

**Sound:** a click on the tap, light whooshes between screens, a success chime on "Order placed".

## 7. The result (1:00 to 1:04)

**Picture:** the full before-and-after slide: "From a few hours to about 5 minutes a day". The
Before row fades back and "A few hours" is struck through. The After row builds: 35 people pick in
the app → The app groups the picks → Host confirms → **about 5 min, every day** (in blue).

**Narrator:** From a few hours a day, to about five minutes.

## 8. Coming soon: local menus (1:04 to 1:10)

**Picture:** a new page slides in. A blue "Coming soon" tag, then "Add your local menu." and "For
the canteen down the street, even if it isn't on Swiggy." A printed paper menu drops in on the
right; its dishes move one by one into a Lunch Board card called "The corner canteen" (veg thali,
masala dosa, lemon rice, filter coffee, each with the clay-pot placeholder), and a blue "Local" tag
pops up.

**Narrator:** Coming soon: add menus from local places, even ones that aren't on Swiggy.

**Sound:** a whoosh in, a pop per dish, a marimba note on "Local".

## 9. End card (1:10 to 1:15)

**Picture:** white. The Lunch Board mark, "Lunch Board", "Everyone picks. One order."

**Narrator:** Lunch Board. Everyone picks. One order.

**Sound:** the music lands on one last ringing chord and fades out.
