How a food party runs.

  1. 1

    1Start food party

    The host offers the restaurants near the office, or chooses them, and sets the cutoff. One link goes out to the team.

  2. 2

    2Review & lock

    Picks arrive on the live board, and people can change their mind until 12:30. The host sees what everyone picked, then locks the picks so the cart stays as it is.

  3. 3

    3Place order

    The host confirms once. Each restaurant's dishes and cooking notes are listed, so the host can add them to Swiggy and place the order. Automatic ordering is coming, once Swiggy approves it.

  4. 4

    4Collect

    The food comes in one restaurant at a time. The host checks each bag against what was ordered and marks it as arrived.

  5. 5

    5Distribute

    Each person's card shows exactly what they ordered, so the right bag goes to the right person. The host ticks them off as they collect.

  6. 6

    6Enjoy your meal

    With everyone served, the host closes the food party so the next one can start. Then everyone eats.
